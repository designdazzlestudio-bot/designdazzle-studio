Our story

Design Dazzle Studio has been running since 2023.

Design Dazzle Studio is a digital design and web development platform focused on helping individuals, businesses, organizations and new brands build a strong online presence at affordable prices.

The platform is run by Wasib Hussain Shah, owner of Design Dazzle Studio and a currently enrolled Software Engineering student. The studio combines practical development skills with visual design to deliver websites and creative work that feel clear, useful and human-made.
